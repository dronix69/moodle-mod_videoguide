# Video Guide

A Moodle activity that lets you share a list of videos with your students and keep track of who has watched them.

## What is it?

**Video Guide** is a new type of activity you can add to any Moodle course. It works like a curated playlist: you collect videos from YouTube, Zoom or Google Meet in one place, and your students watch them at their own pace. Moodle keeps a record of which videos each student has marked as watched, so you always know who is keeping up.

## Built for institutions without paid platforms

Video Guide was made with **technical and vocational institutions** in mind — schools, training centres and institutes that often **don't have licences for Zoom Premium, Google Meet or similar paid platforms**.

You don't need them. A teacher can simply:

1. **Record their class with any device** — a phone, a laptop webcam, a screen recording.
2. **Upload the video to YouTube** (which is free).
3. **Paste the link into the guide** and choose the platform it comes from.

Your students get the same experience they would with an expensive video platform: a tidy list of class videos, progress tracking and required content — while the institution spends nothing on licences.

That makes Video Guide a practical way to turn recorded classes into **content for student development**: lectures, walkthroughs, lab demos or study advice that students can revisit as many times as they need, inside the course they are already using.

## What can teachers do?

- **Create a guide.** Add as many videos as you like to a single activity, each with a title, a link and an optional description.
- **Choose the platform.** Tell Moodle where each video comes from (YouTube, Zoom or Google Meet) so students see a clear badge next to every entry.
- **Mark videos as required.** Highlight the must-watch videos so students don't miss them.
- **Hide or show videos.** Keep upcoming videos hidden and reveal them whenever you want — no need to delete and re-add them.
- **Decide how videos open.** When your students click a video, it can open:
  - in a **new browser tab** (the default),
  - in a **pop-up window** right on the page, or
  - in a **floating window** they can move and resize while continuing to browse the course.
- **Follow the class at a glance.** The same page shows a progress bar, so you can spot which students still need to catch up.

## What do students see?

When a student opens the guide they see a friendly list of the videos, with:

- a **progress bar** showing how many of the videos they have already watched,
- a **check button** next to each video to mark it as watched (or unwatch it by mistake),
- clear **platform badges** (YouTube, Zoom, Google Meet) so they know what to expect,
- a small **exclamation mark** on the videos their teacher marked as required.

Every time a student watches a video and checks it off, the progress bar moves — a simple way to stay motivated and finish the whole guide.

## How do I create and configure a Video Guide?

### 1. Add the activity

1. Go to your course and click **Turn editing on**.
2. In the section where you want the guide, click **Add an activity or resource**.
3. Choose **Video Guide**.

### 2. Fill in the general settings

- **Guide Name** — give the guide a clear title, e.g. "Week 3 – Trigonometry classes".
- **Description** — optional text explaining what students will find and what is expected of them.
- **Video opening method** — choose how the videos will open for your students:
  - **New browser tab** — the classic behaviour; each video opens in its own tab.
  - **Pop-up window (embedded)** — the video opens in a window on top of the page, without leaving the course. Works best with YouTube.
  - **Floating window (browser pop-up)** — a separate window students can move and resize while they follow along with the material.

### 3. Add your videos

In the **Videos** section, complete one card per video:

- **Title** — the name students will see, e.g. "Class 4 – Derivatives explained".
- **Video URL** — paste the link copied from YouTube, Zoom or Google Meet.
- **Platform** — tell Moodle which service the video comes from so students see the right badge.
- **Description** — an optional short note about what the video covers.
- **Visibility** — choose **Visible** to publish the video, or **Hidden** to keep it unpublished until the right moment.
- **Required** — tick this box for must-watch videos; students will see a clear marker on them.

Click **Add Video** to create another card, and **Remove** to delete one.

### 4. Save and finish

Click **Save and display** to see the guide exactly as your students will see it. You can come back and edit everything later — add new videos, hide ones you're not ready to share, or change how the videos open.

That's it — the guide is ready for your students.
